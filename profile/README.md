### 🌟 About Us

Welcome to **FrozenLab**! We design and maintain modular open-source software with a relentless focus on clean code, strong typing, zero unnecessary dependencies, and exceptional developer experience (DX). 

Our tools are built in the open to solve real-world problems faced by modern software teams.

### 🛡️ Core Engineering Principles

- **Zero Bloat**: Lightweight, dependency-conscious implementations that keep bundle sizes minimal.
- **Intuitive Developer Experience**: Declarative and ergonomic APIs that make complex workflows feel natural.
- **Production-Ready & Reliable**: Thoroughly tested with unit, widget, and integration test suites.
- **Open Standards**: Fully documented, transparent roadmaps, and community-first governance.

### 🤝 Contributing & Community

We believe open source thrives on collaboration. Whether you're fixing a bug, proposing a new feature, or improving documentation, your contributions are always welcome!

- 🐛 **Report a Bug**: Check the repository's issue tracker or use our organization-wide bug report templates.
- 💡 **Request a Feature**: Share ideas or use cases in our project discussions or issue trackers.
- 📖 **Code of Conduct**: All contributors and participants are expected to adhere to our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/frozenlabx/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- 🔐 **Security**: Found a security vulnerability? Please read our [Security Policy](https://github.com/frozenlabx/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) for private reporting guidelines.
